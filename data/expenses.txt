Acme Corp Expense Policy
Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.
Daily meal allowance during travel is 1,500 INR in metro cities and 1,000 INR elsewhere.
Expense claims must be filed within 30 days of the expense with original receipts attached. Claims above 10,000 INR need director approval.
Reimbursements are paid in the next monthly payroll cycle.
