Acme Corp Leave Policy
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year; anything above that expires on March 31.
Sick leave is separate: 12 days per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive days.
Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver, fully paid.
Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for sick leave.
