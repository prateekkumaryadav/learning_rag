Acme Corp Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are mandatory in-office days for all teams.
Working from another city for more than 2 weeks requires written approval from your manager and HR.
Working from another country is not allowed for more than 30 days a year because of tax and compliance rules.
The company provides a one-time home office allowance of 25,000 INR for a chair and desk.
